YOUR LOCAL STUDIO

A little clarity.

Clearcut processes images on this server with an open-source AI model. Images and results stay on its disk until you remove the workspace data.

Upload up to 10,000 JPG, PNG or WebP files per batch. Each image can be up to 25 MB and 25 megapixels. Jobs run sequentially on CPU; large batches can take hours and need enough disk space.

Pause takes effect after the current image. Completed batches download in ZIP parts of up to 250 images. Closing the page does not stop processing; keep the server running.

This is a single-user workspace, not a public multi-user service. Results vary with the image, especially hair, glass and fine edges.
